# HereToo — The Paradigm

*Supersedes the family-first thesis in STRATEGY.md and the grandmother
milestones in heretoo-source-of-truth.md. Written July 2026, after the
pivot. Grounded in a four-lane read of Cameron's fiction, his campaign,
this platform's own design docs, and his public work. The evidence
sits in the workflow journal; the conclusions sit here.*

## The finding

Four separate bodies of work, one voice. The children's CYOA, the
serial-killer procedural, the pirate libretto, the Senate campaign, and
this platform's design docs all run on the same engine: deadpan
delivery, procedural competence, object provenance, class solidarity,
refusals stated plainly, Providence down to the street corner.

So stop designing for an audience. The audience was always a costume.
HereToo is one writer's city, built as software. People are invited in
the way regulars find a good bar: told by a person, or they walk past
and something odd catches the eye. Nothing here performs for strangers.

## The image that governs

**The bar with no sign.** From the fiction: the bar pays in beer, the
regulars simply know, the cafe advertises "accuracy" instead of
atmosphere, the door is the education. That is the whole growth model
and the whole aesthetic. No funnel. No onboarding tour. No pitch page
begging a demographic. A place with rooms, run by a particular mind,
worth telling someone about.

## Style paradigm

**Every surface is a worn medium.** His aesthetics are salvage:
cassettes worn thin, VHS tracking errors, zines, butcher paper, foxed
mirrors, CRT phosphor. The generation skins already gesture at this.
Name it and finish it. Each skin is a media format, loved for its
damage:

| Skin | Medium | Now | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alpha | CRT glitch, phosphor, scanline | shipped, default | done, deepen |
| Z | Y2K plastic, early-web chrome | starter palette | vaporwave, pixel dither |
| Millennial | VHS, camcorder date-stamp | starter palette | tracking error, tape label type |
| X | xerox zine, punk flyer | starter palette | photocopy grain, staple, cut-paste |
| Boomer | letterpress, rag paper | shipped (the warm look) | done |

The Shakespearean Insults module set the standard: First Folio print,
scoped, correct in its details. Every room gets that level of media
fidelity or it does not ship.

**The copy register is the narrator's.** Even temperature under all
conditions. Corpses and confetti narrated the same. Short declaratives
after long build. No exclamation marks (already law). The platform is
never sorry, never a cheerleader, never a brand. When something breaks:
state it, offer the next move. When something is delightful: let it be
delightful without announcing it.

## Content paradigm

**Provenance is sacred.** In the fiction every object carries a
micro-biography: the pews from a Cranston church, the mug from
Portland. In the campaign every claim links its bill. In the insult
forge every word cites its play. This is the platform's deepest habit
and it becomes a primitive: things carry where they came from. Posts
can attach receipts. Artifacts name their makers. Nothing is generated
and passed off as written.

**Testimony beats propaganda.** The libretto's antagonist is a book.
Mary refutes the official history page by page. The platform takes the
same side: human authorship stamps, marginalia over erasure, LLM
crawlers blocked because essays are gifts. The Parlor's next editorial
direction is this: testimony, counter-narrative, craft, Providence.

**Both ends of time, chosen per object.** Letters that open in twenty
years. Drops that burn on reading. The author picks the lifespan.
What the platform refuses is the middle: the ambient, permanent,
searchable public record that every other network defaults to.

## Feature paradigm

The old seven-question filter had two cohort questions (the 65-year-old,
the 14-year-old). Those retire. The new gates:

1. **Does it have provenance?** Every object can say where it came from.
2. **Is it earned?** Discovery is walked to, coded in, chained behind,
   or told to you by a person. Never fed by an algorithm.
3. **Is the craft correct?** If the software cannot verify the claim
   (GPS gate, meter scansion, RLS), do not make the claim.
4. **Does it choose its time?** A letter or a drop. Lasting or burning.
   The author decides. No ambient archive.
5. **Would a regular find it funny?** Deadpan, pomposity meeting
   physics, the joke never announced.
6. **Does it stay quiet?** Survives from the old filter, word for word.
   No streaks, no nudges, no anxiety architecture.

**Build next, in rough order of pull:**
- **Meme studio.** Port the campaign meme tool into the app. He built
  it once already: brand-locked templates, in-browser, nothing uploaded.
  A room where you make the thing and take it with you.
- **Provenance fields on posts.** Where the object came from, who made
  it, one line. The "biography of stuff" as UI.
- **Receipts.** A post can attach its primary document. Show me the bill.
- **Serialized fiction primitives.** Chapter drops, branching choice
  trees. The Jude story.json engine already models this. The platform
  becomes a place his fiction (and others') is read serially, with the
  Playhouse bots as precedent for characters who talk back.
- **Marginalia.** Annotate a shared text. Refute it in the margins.
  Counter-narrative as a feature, from Mary vs. the General History.
- **Mixtape exchange.** A gift, not a feed. The rabbit traded for
  Ziggy Stardust. Ties into the music plan when it lands.
- **Craft logs.** "Again next time." Recurring practice, recipes,
  procedures, done correctly, logged without streaks or badges.
- **Providence anchor.** The city is the universe in everything he
  writes. Deaddrop already walks the streets. Lean local: routes,
  regulars, places that require showing up.

**What survives from the family era, reframed as rooms:**
memoir and the book pipeline, letters, subjects, wallpapers, the radio,
the art between posts, video calls. They were never demographic
features. They were rooms in the house, and the house stands.

**Standing refusals, unchanged:** no ads, no data sale, no engagement
metrics, no For You feed, no AI-written posts, no resurrection bots,
no surveillance SDKs. Safety stays in the software, never the pitch.

## Politics, non-ideological

The platform's politics are structural, same as his: it changes what
the network is paid to do, not what its members believe. Mutual aid
over extraction. Surplus people finding each other. The unhoused
brothers hold their profit share. Free is the current price of
everything. The compliant money rail (DEADDROP_PAYMENTS.md) exists so
that when money moves, it moves with receipts.

## One line

A bar with no sign, run by a writer, in Providence, on the internet.
