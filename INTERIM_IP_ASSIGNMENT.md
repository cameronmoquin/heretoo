# HereToo — Interim Intellectual Property Assignment

**Effective Date:** ____________________

This is a binding interim agreement signed during the "lemonade-stand" period — before HereToo LLC has been formally organized — to ensure that all intellectual property contributed to the HereToo project flows cleanly into the Company once it is formed.

---

## Parties

- **Cameron Moquin** ("Cameron")
- **[Brother 1 Full Name]** ("Brother 1")
- **[Brother 2 Full Name]** ("Brother 2")

Collectively the "Contributors."

---

## 1. The Project

"HereToo" is the family-first social platform currently operating at heretoo.social, in the GitHub repository `cameronmoquin/heretoo`, and on the iOS and Android app stores (preview / development builds). It includes the codebase, the HereToo brand mark and logo, the heretoo.social domain, all written copy, all designs, all database migrations, and all derivative works — collectively, the "Project IP."

---

## 2. Future Entity

The Contributors intend to form **HereToo LLC** (the "Future Entity") on or before the date of the first paying ad sale, or by **[calendar date]**, whichever comes first. Upon formation, the Future Entity will operate the HereToo project under the Founders' Equity Agreement separately drafted by the parties.

---

## 3. Assignment

Each Contributor hereby irrevocably assigns to the Future Entity, effective immediately upon the Future Entity's formation, all right, title, and interest in any contribution they have made or will make to the Project IP between the date of their first contribution and the formation of the Future Entity.

This assignment includes, without limitation:

- All copyrights in source code, written copy, designs, and creative work
- All rights in the HereToo brand mark, name, and logo
- The heretoo.social domain registration (currently held by Cameron)
- All trade secrets and know-how related to the platform
- All accounts, repositories, configurations, and digital assets used in operating HereToo
- Any patents, trademark rights, or other intellectual property rights now or hereafter recognized in any jurisdiction with respect to the Project IP

Pending the formation of the Future Entity, Cameron holds the Project IP in trust for the benefit of the Contributors as future Members of the Future Entity.

---

## 4. Non-Interference

Until the Future Entity is formed and the Founders' Equity Agreement is executed, no Contributor will:

- Transfer, license, sell, or encumber any portion of the Project IP to any third party
- File for trademark, patent, or copyright registration on Project IP in their own name
- Operate a competing platform using any portion of the Project IP

---

## 5. Compensation

This is an interim assignment. The Contributors will receive their respective membership interests in the Future Entity per the separately-drafted Founders' Equity Agreement (currently anticipated at 60% Cameron / 20% Brother 1 / 20% Brother 2, with Cameron holding the sole voting class). This Agreement does not create any compensation obligation independent of that structure.

---

## 6. If the Future Entity Is Not Formed

If the Future Entity has not been formed by the deadline in Section 2 — and the deadline has not been extended by unanimous written agreement — this assignment becomes void, and each Contributor retains the rights in their own pre-formation contributions. Project IP that was solely Cameron's work prior to this Agreement remains Cameron's.

---

## 7. Confidentiality

Each Contributor agrees to keep confidential all non-public information about the HereToo project, its strategy, its users (existing or future), its technology, and its finances. This obligation survives any termination of this Agreement and continues for **3 years** after the Future Entity is formed (or after this Agreement is terminated under Section 6).

---

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of **[home state]**.

---

## 9. Severability

If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder remains in force.

---

## 10. Entire Agreement (Interim)

This is the entire interim agreement between the Contributors regarding intellectual property. It will be superseded by the Founders' Equity Agreement and the Operating Agreement of the Future Entity once those are executed.

*This is a working draft, not legal advice. For a small family LLC like this one, an interim IP assignment of this scope is generally considered low-risk and can be signed in good faith pending formal entity formation. If you want the most polished version possible, have a startup attorney review before signing — but in practice, a clearly-written one-pager like this is much better than nothing, and most attorneys would call it adequate for the lemonade-stand stage.*
