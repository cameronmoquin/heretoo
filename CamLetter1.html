A letter from your neighbor, Cameron Moquin. I'd like to introduce myself.

Cameron Moquin  ·  RI Senate District 5

Hi neighbor,

My name is Cameron Moquin. I'm running for Rhode Island Senate in District 5. Over the next few months I'll be walking the West End, Federal Hill, the Valley, and Elmhurst.

I'm writing to ask permission to knock on your door.

I'm a progressive. I believe the world is a better place when we lift each other up. I also know that progressive ideas only become law when people across the ideological spectrum agree they're worth doing. The things we want are necessary. They're worthwhile. They're achievable when we work together.

There is real division among Democrats right now. I'm not interested in talking past anyone. I want to hear how you actually feel about the cost of groceries, the price at the pump, the healthcare system, the quality of our children's education, and the thing that matters most to you. The constant crisis makes it easy to lose sight of the things that hit your household every week.

I want to talk about it. In person.

 

Three ways we can meet

1. I knock on your door.

Pick a day and a time window. I'll be there. No script.

2. Meet me for coffee.

Open conversations at local coffee shops on set dates. Bring your questions.

3. Invite me to your gathering.

House party, block party, book club. Put the thumbscrews to me. Test my stripes.
