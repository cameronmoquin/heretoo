What's the FSOT?

The Foreign Service Officer Test is the U.S. State Department's entrance exam for diplomats. It tests job knowledge — US history and government, world history and affairs, economics, geography, math and statistics — plus English expression and logical reasoning, and it's the first gate in the Foreign Service selection process.
