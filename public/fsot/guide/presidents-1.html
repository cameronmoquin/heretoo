The presidents in runs, Washington to Lincoln

US History · 3 min read

Learn the presidents as five short runs, not one list of 45. Each run has a story.

Run one, the Founders' Republic (1789 to 1825): Washington, Adams, Jefferson, Madison, Monroe. Mnemonic: Will A Jolly Man Make. One tag each: Washington keeps out of Europe's wars; Adams signs the Alien and Sedition Acts; Jefferson buys Louisiana; Madison fights the War of 1812; Monroe issues the Doctrine.

Run two, the Jacksonian era (1825 to 1849): John Quincy Adams, Jackson, Van Buren, Harrison, Tyler, Polk. Mnemonic: Another Jolly Victory Has Ten Points. Tags: JQ Adams loses the 'corrupt bargain' rematch; Jackson kills the Bank and removes the Cherokee; Van Buren gets the Panic of 1837; Harrison dies in a month; Tyler annexes Texas; Polk takes the Mexican Cession and Oregon.

Run three, the road to war (1849 to 1861): Taylor, Fillmore, Pierce, Buchanan. Mnemonic: Tired Feet Please Buchanan. Tags: Taylor dies in office; Fillmore signs the 1850 Compromise; Pierce signs Kansas-Nebraska; Buchanan watches secession and does nothing.

Then Lincoln, the sixteenth. Everything before him is a slope toward war; everything after starts from the ruins.
