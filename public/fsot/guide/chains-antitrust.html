Chain: regulating business

US Government · 1 min read

Interstate Commerce Act 1887 (railroads, the ICC, first regulatory agency). Sherman Antitrust 1890 (Harrison signs; vague). Northern Securities 1904 (TR uses Sherman). Standard Oil broken up 1911 (Taft). Clayton Act and FTC 1914 (Wilson; unions exempted). Glass-Steagall 1933 (banks split). Wagner Act 1935 (unions). Fair Labor Standards 1938 (minimum wage). Sarbanes-Oxley 2002 (accounting). Dodd-Frank 2010 (banks again).

The trap: Teddy Roosevelt used the Sherman Act; he didn't write it.
