The eras in one breath

US History · 2 min read

Nine eras, each with a decade range and a single defining tension. If you can place a question in its era, you can usually eliminate two options.

Colonial and Revolution (to 1783): taxation and representation. Founding and Early Republic (1783 to 1825): how strong should the national government be? Antebellum (1820 to 1860): slavery in the territories. Civil War and Reconstruction (1861 to 1877): union, then who counts as a citizen. Gilded Age (1877 to 1900): industry versus farmers and labor. Progressive Era and WWI (1900 to 1920): reforming capitalism. Boom, Bust, and WWII (1920 to 1945): the state steps in. Cold War (1945 to 1991): containment. Modern (1991 on): terrorism, trade, and polarization.

Dating heuristic: a question about a law's 'first' of anything (first regulatory agency, first income tax, first peacetime draft) is almost always Gilded Age, Progressive, or 1940.
