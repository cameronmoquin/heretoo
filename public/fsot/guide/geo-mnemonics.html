Geography that scores

World History & Geography · 2 min read

Chokepoints, west to east: Panama, Gibraltar, Bosporus, Suez, Bab el-Mandeb, Hormuz, Malacca, Taiwan Strait. Each links two named waters: Gibraltar (Atlantic to Mediterranean), Bosporus (Marmara to Black Sea), Suez (Mediterranean to Red Sea), Bab el-Mandeb (Red Sea to Gulf of Aden), Hormuz (Persian Gulf to Gulf of Oman), Malacca (Indian Ocean to South China Sea).

Capitals that are not the biggest city: Canberra, Ottawa, Wellington, Brasília, Abuja, Ankara, Islamabad, Rabat, Bern, Pretoria, Dodoma, Naypyidaw, Astana, Sucre-La Paz, Yamoussoukro. If the famous city is an option, it's usually the distractor.

Frozen conflicts by patron: Russia backs Transnistria, South Ossetia, Abkhazia, and did Nagorno-Karabakh's Armenian side; Turkey backs Northern Cyprus; Morocco holds Western Sahara; Somaliland stands alone. Island disputes: Senkaku (Japan-China), Kurils (Japan-Russia), Spratlys and Paracels (China and neighbors), Dokdo (Korea-Japan).

Landlocked traps: Bolivia, Paraguay, Nepal, Bhutan, Laos, Mongolia, Afghanistan, all five Central Asian stans (Kazakhstan touches the Caspian only), Ethiopia since 1993, Switzerland, Austria, Czechia, Hungary, Serbia.
