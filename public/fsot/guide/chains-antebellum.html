Chain: the road to the Civil War

US History · 2 min read

Each deal undoes the last. Say it as a staircase.

1820 Missouri Compromise draws a line at 36°30′. 1832 Nullification: South Carolina tests federal power over tariffs and loses. 1846 Wilmot Proviso tries to ban slavery in the Mexican Cession and fails. 1850 Compromise trades a free California for a harsher fugitive slave law. 1854 Kansas-Nebraska erases the line for popular sovereignty and births the Republican Party. 1856 Bleeding Kansas and the caning of Sumner. 1857 Dred Scott says Congress never had the power to draw the line. 1858 Lincoln-Douglas. 1859 Harpers Ferry. 1860 Lincoln elected with 40 percent; South Carolina secedes in December. April 1861 Fort Sumter.

Presidents through it: Monroe, JQ Adams, Jackson, Van Buren, Harrison, Tyler, Polk, Taylor, Fillmore, Pierce, Buchanan, Lincoln.
