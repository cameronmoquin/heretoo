Fast calculation without paper

Math, Logic & English · 3 min read

On the at-home test the scratchpad is a text box. Work from the answer choices: estimate to one figure, eliminate, compute exactly only if two options are close.

Percent of a number: 10 percent is a decimal shift, 5 percent is half of that, 1 percent is two shifts. Flip when easier: 8 percent of 25 equals 25 percent of 8.

Percent change: divide by the starting number. Up from 80 to 100 is 25 percent; down from 100 to 80 is 20 percent. Sequential changes don't cancel: down 50 then up 50 lands at 75.

Percentage points versus percent: 4 to 5 is one point and a 25 percent increase. If both appear as options, that distinction is the question.

Doubling: rule of 70. Compounding for a few years: add the rates, then add a little. Real equals nominal minus inflation. Per capita growth equals GDP growth minus population growth.

Ratios: a 3 to 5 split is 3 of 8 parts. Know the eighths (12.5, 25, 37.5, 50, 62.5, 75, 87.5) and the sixths (16.7, 33.3, 50, 66.7, 83.3).

Division: strip matching zeros. 'X is 2 percent of what' means multiply by 50. Margin of error shrinks with the square root of the sample: four times the sample halves it. Weighted averages lean toward the larger group.
