The world history spine

World History & Geography · 3 min read

Twelve anchors, one per century or era, so any world question can be placed relative to something you know.

1215 Magna Carta. 1453 Constantinople falls, Byzantium ends. 1492 Columbus; 1494 Tordesillas. 1517 Luther. 1648 Westphalia, sovereign states. 1688 Glorious Revolution. 1789 French Revolution. 1815 Waterloo and Vienna. 1848 revolutions. 1871 Germany unified. 1914 to 1918 WWI; 1917 Russia. 1939 to 1945 WWII. 1947 India, 1948 Israel, 1949 China. 1960 Africa's year. 1989 the Wall. 1991 the USSR ends.

Same-year pairs the test likes: 1776 (Declaration, Wealth of Nations), 1848 (Communist Manifesto, revolutions, Seneca Falls), 1861 (US Civil War begins, Russian serfs freed), 1871 (Germany, Paris Commune), 1898 (Spain loses Cuba, Philippines; Fashoda), 1917 (US enters WWI, Bolsheviks), 1947 (Truman Doctrine, Marshall Plan, India), 1979 (Iran, Afghanistan, Thatcher, China's opening). If two options sit in the same year, the question is about which event, not which year.
