The presidents, Lincoln to Hoover

US History · 3 min read

The same traditional mnemonic keeps going, and this stretch is where it earns its keep: it is the version that gets Cleveland right.

Long John Grant Had Gone And Chased His Crazy Mooses, 'Round The West Hollering, Can't Help. Long is Lincoln (sixteen), John is Johnson (Andrew, seventeen), Grant is Grant (eighteen), Had is Hayes (nineteen), Gone is Garfield (twenty), And is Arthur (twenty-one), Chased is Cleveland (twenty-two), His is Harrison (Benjamin, twenty-three), Crazy is Cleveland again (twenty-four), Mooses is McKinley (twenty-five).

Cleveland is the trap. He is the only president to serve two non-consecutive terms, so he is counted twice, twenty-two and twenty-four, with Benjamin Harrison wedged between. Chased His Crazy: Cleveland, Harrison, Cleveland. Get that sandwich wrong and every number after it is off by one.

Then: 'Round is Roosevelt (Theodore, twenty-six), The is Taft (twenty-seven), West is Wilson (twenty-eight), Hollering is Harding (twenty-nine), Can't is Coolidge (thirty), Help is Hoover (thirty-one).

Tags: Lincoln, the war; Andrew Johnson, impeached; Grant, scandals and the Klan Acts; Hayes, troops leave the South; Garfield, shot by an office-seeker; Arthur, signs civil service reform; Cleveland, the split-term president; Benjamin Harrison, signs the Sherman Act; McKinley, the Spanish-American War, then shot.

Tags, continued: TR, Square Deal and the canal; Taft, more trust-busting and the 1912 split; Wilson, the Fed and WWI; Harding, Teapot Dome; Coolidge, the business of America is business; Hoover, the Crash.

Drill

Tap the presidents from Lincoln to Hoover in order. Cleveland serves twice, around Benjamin Harrison.
