The presidents, Lincoln to Hoover

US History · 3 min read

Run four, war and the Gilded Age (1861 to 1901): Lincoln, Johnson, Grant, Hayes, Garfield, Arthur, Cleveland, Harrison, Cleveland, McKinley. Mnemonic: Lincoln Just Governs Heartily; Garfield And Chester Come Home, McKinley.

Tags: Lincoln, the war; Andrew Johnson, impeached; Grant, scandals and the Klan Acts; Hayes, troops leave the South; Garfield, shot by an office-seeker; Arthur, signs civil service reform; Cleveland, the only split-term president; Benjamin Harrison, signs the Sherman Act; McKinley, the Spanish-American War, then shot.

Run five, Progressives to the Crash (1901 to 1933): T. Roosevelt, Taft, Wilson, Harding, Coolidge, Hoover. Mnemonic: Teddy Takes Wilson's Hat; Cal Hoovers.

Tags: TR, Square Deal and the canal; Taft, more trust-busting and the 1912 split; Wilson, the Fed and WWI; Harding, Teapot Dome; Coolidge, the business of America is business; Hoover, the Crash.
