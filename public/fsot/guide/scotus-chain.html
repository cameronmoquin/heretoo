Chain: the Supreme Court by era

US Government · 3 min read

Marshall Court (1801 to 1835) builds federal power: Marbury 1803 (judicial review), McCulloch 1819 (implied powers, no state tax on the Bank), Gibbons 1824 (commerce), Worcester 1832 (Cherokee sovereignty, ignored).

Taney Court: Dred Scott 1857. Post-war: Slaughter-House 1873 (14th narrowed), Civil Rights Cases 1883 (no reach to private acts), Plessy 1896 (separate but equal), Lochner 1905 (liberty of contract), Schenck 1919 (clear and present danger).

New Deal: Schechter 1935 strikes the NRA; West Coast Hotel 1937 is the switch in time; Wickard 1942 stretches commerce; Korematsu 1944 upholds internment.

Warren Court (1953 to 1969) expands rights: Brown 1954, Mapp 1961, Engel 1962, Gideon 1963, Sullivan 1964, Griswold 1965, Miranda 1966, Loving 1967, Tinker 1969. Burger Court: Pentagon Papers 1971, Roe 1973, US v. Nixon 1974, Buckley 1976, Bakke 1978.

Rehnquist and Roberts Courts pull back: Lopez 1995 (commerce limit), Bush v. Gore 2000, Heller 2008, Citizens United 2010, NFIB 2012 (ACA upheld), Shelby County 2013, Obergefell 2015, Rucho 2019, Dobbs 2022, West Virginia v. EPA 2022, SFFA 2023, Loper Bright 2024, Trump v. US 2024.
