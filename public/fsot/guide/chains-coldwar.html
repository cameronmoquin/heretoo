Chain: the Cold War

US History · 2 min read

1946 Kennan's Long Telegram. 1947 Truman Doctrine, Marshall Plan, National Security Act. 1948 to 49 Berlin Airlift. 1949 NATO; China goes communist; Soviet bomb. 1950 NSC-68; Korea. 1953 armistice; Stalin dies. 1955 Warsaw Pact. 1956 Suez; Hungary. 1957 Sputnik. 1959 Castro. 1961 Bay of Pigs; Berlin Wall. 1962 Cuban Missile Crisis. 1964 Tonkin. 1968 Tet; Prague Spring. 1972 Nixon in China; SALT I. 1973 Paris accords; War Powers. 1975 Saigon falls. 1979 Iran, Afghanistan. 1983 SDI. 1985 Gorbachev. 1987 INF Treaty. 1989 Wall falls. 1991 USSR dissolves.

Anchor rule: anything with 'Berlin' is 1948, 1961, or 1989. Anything with 'Cuba' is 1959 to 1962.
