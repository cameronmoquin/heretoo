Chain: conscription in America

US History · 1 min read

A confirmed test topic. Six dates carry the whole thread.

April 1862 the Confederacy drafts first (ages 18 to 35, later 17 to 50, with the Twenty Negro exemption). March 1863 the Union Enrollment Act (ages 20 to 45, $300 commutation or a substitute); the New York draft riots follow in July. 1917 Selective Service Act for WWI. September 1940 the first peacetime draft. 1948 the draft is reinstated after the 1940 act lapsed and runs continuously. 1969 the lottery replaces local-board deferments. 1973 the draft ends; the all-volunteer force begins. 1980 registration is restored after the Soviet invasion of Afghanistan.

Read the stem: 'first draft in the US' points to 1862; 'first federal draft' points to 1863; 'first peacetime draft' is 1940.
