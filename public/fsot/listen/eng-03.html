Transcript

Episode forty-seven: the English gauntlet — twenty sentences at speed; call each one clean or flawed, name the flaw, fix it aloud. The rules from episode twenty-eight are all in play: agreement, pronouns, modifiers, parallelism, splices, confusables. Beat, verdict, my answer. Go. One. The committee, along with its advisers, were unable to agree. Flawed — the committee was unable; along with is packaging. Two. Neither the consul nor the officers was informed of the change. Flawed — nearer subject rules: the officers were informed. Three. Whoever the ambassador designates will chair the meeting. Clean — designates him: whomever? No — the whole clause is subject; inside it, whoever... wait. Actually: the ambassador designates him — object — so whomever. Flawed, subtly: whomever the ambassador designates will chair. The clause chairs; inside the clause, the designated one is an object. The hardest pronoun case on the test — decide inside the clause. Four. Each of the embassies have submitted their security plans. Doubly flawed — each has submitted its plan: singular verb, singular pronoun. Five. Rushing to meet the deadline, several errors crept into the cable. Flawed — dangler: errors don't rush; rushing to meet the deadline, the drafter let errors creep in. Six. The minister demanded that the report be released immediately. Clean — the subjunctive after demand: that it be, not that it is. Demands, recommendations, and requests take be. Seven. The new policy will effect significant changes in visa processing. Clean — effect as a verb means bring about; to affect changes would mean merely influencing them. The one place effect-the-verb is right. Eight. Between you and I, the negotiations are going poorly. Flawed — between you and me; prepositions take object case. Nine. The delegation visited Vienna, toured the agency, and briefings were attended. Flawed — parallelism: visited, toured, and attended briefings. Ten. The data was compiled by three separate teams. Test-flawed — formal English: the data were compiled. Datum, data; criterion, criteria; phenomenon, phenomena — all take plural verbs on the exam. Eleven. Having reviewed both proposals, the second seems stronger. Flawed — dangler: having reviewed both proposals, I find the second stronger; the seeming can't do the reviewing. Twelve. The consulate processed less applications this quarter than last. Flawed — fewer applications; applications count. Thirteen. The treaty, which was signed in Geneva, and which required decades of negotiation, entering into force next year. Flawed — no main verb: entering should be enters; the whiches stranded the sentence. Fragment by relative-clause pileup. Fourteen. If the senator would have voted yes, the treaty would have passed. Flawed — if the senator had voted yes; the if-clause takes had, never would have. Fifteen. The ambassador is one of those officers who believes in cables before calls. Flawed, finely — one of those officers who believe: who reaches back to officers, plural. Officers who believe; she is one of them. Sixteen. Its unclear whether the ministry will honor it's commitments. Doubly flawed, reversed: it's unclear — it is; its commitments — possessive. Both apostrophes are on the wrong words. Seventeen. The report implies that corruption is widespread; readers can only infer the details. Clean — the report implies, readers infer; the pair used correctly. Eighteen. Not only did the minister deny the report, but also refused to meet the press. Flawed — parallel clamps: not only did he deny, but he also refused; the second limb lost its subject. Nineteen. The number of applicants have risen sharply; a number of them is unqualified. Doubly flawed, reversed again: the number has risen — singular; a number of them are unqualified — plural. The number is one; a number are many. Twenty, the closer. Based on the inspector's findings, the post was commended for their preparedness, being that morale and security was excellent. A pileup — three flaws minimum: the post... its preparedness; being that is not standard — because; morale and security were — compound subject. Rebuilt: based on the inspector's findings, the post was commended for its preparedness, because morale and security were excellent. Score yourself: eighteen or better, the section is yours; below fifteen, replay twenty-eight and this gauntlet back to back. The ear you're building — the little trip wire that snags on were where was belongs — is the whole skill: on screen, the error will glow.
