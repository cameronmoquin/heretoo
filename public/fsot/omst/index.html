FSOT / OMST

The OMST track

The Office Management Specialist Test is not the FSOT. It tests English expression, reading comprehension, and situational judgment — and no job knowledge at all.

What that means for this site

The history, government, economics, geography, and math material here is FSOT preparation and is not scored on the OMST. The English material is shared. The logic material transfers to reading comprehension, because both turn on holding an inference to must be true.

One more thing worth knowing: situational judgment was removed from the FSOT in October 2025 and is still on the OMST. Prep written for both tests often calls it obsolete. For the OMST it is a third of the exam.

Practice

The practice app has OMST exam modes under Timed exams: a rehearsal paper, plus separate situational judgment and reading comprehension papers. They draw only on the three sections the OMST actually tests — 296 questions across them.

There is also a dated study plan for the weeks you have left.

You may take the OMST once in any twelve-month period, so there is no reconnaissance attempt. Finish the preparation before you sit it.
