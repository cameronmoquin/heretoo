FSOT / OMST / Plan

The plan to 19 October

31 days. You may take the OMST once in any twelve-month period, so this is built to finish before you sit it, not to peak on the day.

Phase 1 — Method · Thu 17 Sep to Sun 27 Sep

Listen to the six method episodes in order, then the two drill episodes. Work questions untimed and read every explanation, including on the ones you got right: situational judgment is full of items you can get right by instinct once and wrong by instinct twice.

Phase 2 — Timed sections · Sun 27 Sep to Thu 8 Oct

Switch to the clock, one section at a time, and alternate between them rather than blocking. Interleaved practice feels worse and tests better. Thirty situational items in thirty-five minutes; twenty-four reading items in thirty; English under time, fast, because English is where you bank the minutes you spend on passages.

Phase 3 — Rehearsal · Thu 8 Oct to Fri 16 Oct

Two full rehearsals at the real hour, late afternoon, sitting the whole paper without stopping. Sort every wrong answer into three piles: did not know it, misread the stem, got it to two and chose wrong. Replay the method episodes once each.

Phase 4 — Taper · Fri 16 Oct to Sun 18 Oct

Nothing new. Confirm the test centre address and the trip at that hour, lay out your identification, one light pass over the method, and sleep.

The three rules underneath it

Space it. Four half-hour sessions across four days beat one four-hour session, by a wide margin, for anything you need to still have a week later. This is the most replicated finding in the study of memory and the one study plans most often ignore.

Interleave it. Mixing the three sections produces worse practice sessions and better test performance. Blocked practice feels more productive while you are doing it, which is exactly why people choose it.

Retrieve, don't reread. Answering out loud in the pauses is the whole point of the audio episodes. Listening without answering is roughly half the value.
